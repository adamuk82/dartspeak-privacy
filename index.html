Privacy Policy for dARt Speak

dARt Speak stores your account and game history on your iPhone. We do not run an account server and we do not sell your data.

We collect the name, username, email and password you enter, so you can sign in on this device. If you turn on Face ID, it is used only to unlock your sign-in. If you add a profile photo, it is saved with your account on this device.

The microphone and speech recognition are used to hear the score you say. Speech is recognised on your device.

Practice and match history are stored on your device. Deleting the app removes this on-device data. The iOS Keychain may keep your sign-in so you can log in again after a reinstall.
